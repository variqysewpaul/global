Armed response & control room

A control room that never closes. Armed response that’s always on call.

Every alarm, panic button and sensor trigger reaches our control room, which operates around the clock. When a response is needed, armed response vehicles are dispatched day or night.

  1. 01

    Signal received

    Your alarm sends a signal to our control room the moment it is triggered.

  2. 02

    Operator checks

    A control room operator picks up the signal and assesses it.

  3. 03

    Response dispatched

    Marked armed response vehicles are sent out, 24 hours a day, including weekends and public holidays.

  4. 04

    On site

    Responders attend your property. You can also reach the control room directly on 034 315 4237.
