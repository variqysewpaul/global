01Armed response

When an alarm trips, this is what happens.

Our control room is always operating, listening for every trigger. Here’s the path from your alarm to a response vehicle on its way.

  1. 1

    Trigger

    An alarm, panic button or sensor trips at your property and sends a signal to our control room.

  2. 2

    Control room

    The control room is staffed and listening around the clock. An operator picks up the signal and checks it.

  3. 3

    Armed response dispatched

    Marked response vehicles with armed officers are sent out — day or night, weekends and public holidays included.

  4. 4

    On site

    The responders attend your property. You can reach the control room any time on 034 315 4237.
