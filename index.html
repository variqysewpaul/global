Global Security Solutions

At Global Security Solutions we believe real security goes further than hardware. It is knowing that someone is watching, and that someone is on the way. We supply and install alarm systems, gate automation, CCTV and security hardware for homes and businesses in Newcastle and the Amajuba District, and back them with a 24-hour control room and armed response.

Every alarm, panic button and sensor trigger reaches our control room, which operates around the clock. An operator checks the signal and, when a response is needed, armed response vehicles are dispatched, day or night, weekends and public holidays included.

Every property is different. We start with a conversation and a site visit to understand what you need to protect, then design the system around it. Our technicians supply, install and maintain it, and our control room and armed response stand behind it, around the clock.
