01Global Security Solutions

At Global Security Solutions we believe real security goes further than hardware. It is knowing that someone is watching, and that someone is on the way.

We supply and install alarm systems, gate automation, CCTV and security hardware for homes and businesses in Newcastle and the Amajuba District, and back them with a 24-hour control room and armed response.

Every alarm, panic button and sensor trigger reaches our control room, which operates around the clock. An operator checks the signal and, when a response is needed, armed response vehicles are dispatched, day or night, weekends and public holidays included.

06About us

Based in Newcastle. On call 24 hours.

Global Security Solutions is a security installation and services provider based at 89 Scott Street, Newcastle Central. We supply, install and maintain security systems and equipment for homes and businesses across Newcastle and the Amajuba District.

Our technicians supply, install, maintain and integrate alarm systems, gate automation, CCTV and security hardware. We also supply specialised equipment and replacement parts, retail or direct, without installation.

Our control room operates 24 hours a day and armed response is available day and night, including weekends and public holidays. We are based in Newcastle Central and cover Newcastle and the surrounding Amajuba District, with client deployments reaching further across KwaZulu-Natal. Call, email or visit us to arrange a quote or a site visit.
